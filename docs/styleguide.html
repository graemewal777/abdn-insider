05 — Design Rules
✦ Always Do
→ Hard borders only — 3–4px solid black
→ Syne for all display/headline text
→ DM Mono for all body, labels, metadata
→ High contrast — black on parchment or yellow on black
→ Oversized numbers on list slides
→ ALL CAPS for slide titles and CTA text
→ Vibe notes in italic mono, border-left accent
→ Tight leading on headlines (0.9–1.0)
→ Use North Sea teal for new openings only
→ Keep layouts asymmetric and bold
✕ Never Do
× Rounded corners anywhere
× Drop shadows or glows
× Gradients of any kind
× More than 3 colours on one slide
× Light/thin font weights
× Centred layouts on event slides
× Emoji overload — max 1 per slide
× Generic sans-serif fonts (Inter, Roboto, Arial)
× Soft, pastel or muted colour schemes
× Corporate or formal language
